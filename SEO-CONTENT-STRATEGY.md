# BigByte Tech — SEO Content Strategy

**Site:** https://www.bigbytetech.com.au
**Date:** June 2026
**Scope:** Identify content pages that can attract relevant organic traffic from Australian small businesses searching for IT support, VoIP, cloud, automation, and web development services in Brisbane and the Gold Coast.

---

## 1. Current state analysis

### What exists today

- A **single-page site** (`/`) with anchor sections: Services, Web Development, Why Us, About, Process, Contact.
- Good technical foundations: Astro static output, sitemap, canonical tags, OG/Twitter meta, `ProfessionalService` structured data with `areaServed` Brisbane/Gold Coast, `en_AU` locale.
- A 404 page. Nothing else.

### The core problem

A one-page site can realistically rank for **one keyword cluster** (roughly "BigByte Tech" + a weak shot at "IT support small business Brisbane"). Every service — managed IT, VoIP, cloud backup, automation, web development — competes for the same URL, the same title tag, and the same H1. Google has nothing to match against long-tail queries like *"how much does managed IT support cost"* or *"Essential Eight for small business"*, which are exactly the queries your future customers type **before** they search for a vendor.

### The opportunity

1. **Local service pages** — "managed IT support Brisbane" style queries have clear commercial intent and only local MSPs compete for them. A small, well-structured site can rank here.
2. **Pricing and comparison guides** — "how much does X cost in Australia", "VoIP vs landline", "in-house vs managed IT". Buyers search these early; almost no Brisbane MSP answers them honestly. Honest pricing content is a proven differentiator and link magnet.
3. **Australian-specific security content** — the ACSC **Essential Eight**, the **Notifiable Data Breaches scheme**, cyber.gov.au guidance. These are uniquely Australian topics with real search demand and low competition from global content farms (US content doesn't mention them, so Australian-focused pages stand out).
4. **Web development long-tail** — "small business website cost Australia" supports the web development service line.

### Notes on keyword data

Search-volume figures below are qualitative estimates (Low / Low–Medium / Medium) based on topic class — validate with Google Keyword Planner or Search Console once pages are indexed. For a domain of this size, **low-volume + low-competition + high-intent beats high-volume every time.**
